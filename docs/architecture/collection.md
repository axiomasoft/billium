# Billium Collection Module Architecture (`billium-collection`)

The `billium-collection` optional module (`L2`, namespace `Billium\Collection\`, schema stream `billium.collection`) owns versioned saved-method consent proofs (`ConsentBinding`), durable `DunningCase`-rank consent/cancellation guard state and pre-operation tombstones, immutable original-binding refund route provenance, and `requires_action` customer-action receipts. It never stores raw PAN/CVV/card secrets, never runs autonomous provider subscription renewal schedulers, and never owns host entitlements or grants.

## Module & Table Ownership

Dependencies declared in `spec/modules.json` and `CollectionPlugin`:

- **Required modules**: `platform`, `ledger`, `execution`.
- **Required host port**: `CollectionConsentGuard` (`Billium\Collection\Contracts\CollectionConsentGuard`). Unbound default (`UnboundCollectionConsentGuard`) fails closed (`ProblemCode::LookupUnavailable`).
- **Config gate**: `billium.collection.enabled` (`false` by default). Disabled compositions (`embedded-commerz`, `ledger-only`, or omitted/disabled `collection` or missing `execution`) register zero collection providers, workers, or routes.

Owned tables (`public.billium_collection_*`, migrations exclusively under `packages/core/database/migrations/collection/`):

## Core Invariants (P7.1–P7.2)

1. **Proof/Reference Consent Owner without PAN/CVV**: `ConsentBinding` stores opaque provider/vault token locators and external mandate proof digests only. `PanCvvGuard` and SQL CHECK constraints reject PAN-like digit runs, CVV/CVC values, and sensitive keys before persistence.
2. **Merchant & Seller Isolation + Saved-Method Capability**: `SavedMethodCapabilityValidator` requires exact `(sellerRef, environment, merchantAccountRef, merchantConfigRevision)` agreement with `MerchantRevision` and its matching `CapabilitySnapshot`, enforcing `EffectKind::Charge`, `DriverCapability::SavedMethod`, `DriverCapability::RecurringConsent` (for recurring/dunning/usage purposes), currency support, and `QualificationMode::ProviderVerified` in `live` environments. A token bound to another seller or merchant is forbidden for new charges; moving a consent to a new merchant requires a new consent version with fresh verified evidence.
3. **Immutable Proof vs Mutable Guard State**: `billium_collection_consent_binding` is strictly immutable. Current revocation and host cancellation generation live in `billium_collection_consent_guard` + `billium_collection_guard_tombstone`. Revocation or cancellation accepted before any execution operation exists persists durable guard state, `source_revision`, `cancellation_generation`, and a `RevocationReceipt` whose `effectiveAt` timestamp defines when local revocation became effective.
4. **DunningCase-Rank Serialization & Out-of-Transaction Host Authority**: Consent binding supersession, revocation/cancellation, recurring ownership, dunning case transitions, and downstream admission/dispatch validation serialize in strict ascending segment order under `LockRank::DunningCase` (rank 60: segment 0 `consent_guard`, segment 1 `recurring_owner`, segment 2 `dunning_case`) via `LockSet` — never `->lockForUpdate()`. External `CollectionConsentGuard::currentAuthority()` calls and service root entrypoints run strictly outside every database transaction (checked across `transactionLevel()`, `getRawPdo()`, and `getRawReadPdo()`).
5. **Original Refund Route Preservation & `requires_action` Separation**: Rotating an account's consent binding to a new version or merchant never alters `billium_collection_refund_route` for prior charges (`assertRefundRouteMatchesOriginal` refuses new-merchant refund routing). `requires_action` produces a `CustomerActionReceipt` with `paid = false` and `terminalFailure = false`.
6. **Immutable `DunningPolicy` Versions & Explicit Case Migration**: Updating a `DunningPolicy` inserts a new append-only version (`policy_version + 1`) and never modifies any existing `dunning_case`. Moving an existing case to another policy version requires an explicit `DunningCaseService::migrateCasePolicy` command with `expectedCaseRevision` CAS and non-empty `reason`, writing an immutable `billium_collection_case_policy_migration` audit record and rejecting terminal or `parked_unknown` cases. The target decline budget must cover declines already recorded and one possible decline per in-flight step. Existing steps keep the delivery retry limits of the policy versions under which they were created.
7. **Distinct Delivery Retry vs Business Decline Budgets**: Transport/command delivery retries (`recordDeliveryRetry`) consume only the step's `delivery_attempts_used / max_delivery_retries`, preserve the identical `business_payment_intent_ref` and `operation_ref`, and never decrement `business_decline_attempts_used`. Proven terminal declines (`FailedTerminal` with `DeclineCategory` and `outcomeEvidenceDigest`) consume `case.business_decline_attempts_used / max_business_decline_attempts`, evaluate retryability and integer-math backoff against the case's pinned policy, and exhaust the case once non-retryable, over budget, or past `recovery_deadline_at`.
8. **Single Recurring Owner per Agreement Component**: `billium_collection_recurring_owner` enforces at most one active owner per `(seller_ref, environment, component_ref)` via partial unique index and `LockRank::DunningCase` segment 1. Attempting to enable `provider_autonomous_subscription` alongside `billium_scheduler` (or vice versa) rejects deterministically (`billium.collection.recurring_owner_conflict`), and opening a `dunning_case` on a component owned by `provider_autonomous_subscription` rejects deterministically (`billium.collection.provider_autonomous_subscription_forbidden`).
9. **Unknown Prior Step Parking & New Explicit Intent for Next Step**: Transitioning a step to `unknown` atomically parks the parent `dunning_case` (`parked_unknown`), forbidding next-step planning, policy migration, delivery retry, or direct cancellation until reconciled with evidence. After a proven retryable `failed_terminal` decline, planning the next step requires a brand-new explicit `businessPaymentIntentRef` and `operationRef` (`UNIQUE` within seller/environment).

## Collection step authority

- Grant `billium.collection.direct_step_lifecycle` through the host-authenticated `CallerContext` only to integrations trusted to attest host-managed collection outcomes for that seller and environment. Both `planStep` and `transitionStep` require the grant, including on replay. The manual intervention grant governs operator overrides separately.
- `planStep` records a host-managed step and reserves its step and intent identifiers in collection. A supplied `stepAuthorizationRef` is a reference for that lifecycle. Executable collection uses `authorizeCollection`, which checks the consent, recurring owner, merchant, and ledger balance and writes the authorization, settlement intent, and outbox command together.
- For a host-managed step, `transitionStep` applies the caller's lifecycle attestation at the expected step revision. Terminal success and decline require an evidence digest. A success uses the attested `updatedOpenMinor` when provided and otherwise subtracts the recorded step amount from the current case balance, with a floor of zero.
- For executable steps, a direct terminal transition also requires the frozen intent-owner namespace and execution-profile grants. Collection checks the matching execution state and ledger application, then projects the locked ledger balance. The execution guard records dispatch and unsent closure. Only the recovery worker can park an executable step under a current claim.

## Consent cap window

`perPeriodCapMinor` applies per seller, environment, consent reference, and cap currency in the UTC calendar month of authorization issuance. The window includes midnight on the first day of the month and ends before midnight on the first day of the next month. The admission transaction uses the same server timestamp for the cap check and the immutable `issued_at`. Commitments in the window count across binding versions, including in-flight steps and successful collections. Cancelled and terminally declined steps release their commitment. A settled renewal from an earlier month does not use the current month's cap.

## Notification delivery diagnostics

Notification delivery diagnostics contain a typed problem code or the generic `delivery_failed` code. Free-form transport exception text is discarded before storage and filtered when reading earlier rows. An omitted notification payload is stored as an empty JSON object.

## Component cancellation and compensation (P7.5)

- The ProductCoordinator delivers the host cancellation to the financial owners. Schedule advances the component generation and cancels planned intervals while preserving obligated occurrences. Collection accepts a host tombstone for a component-specific consent, or retires the locked recurring owner when the mandate is shared. Retirement blocks new collection for that owner and lets collection close an unadmitted step after an epoch change using the retained owner revision as evidence. (Host, schedule and collection under REQ-NBR-06, REQ-FAIL-19, INV-11 and D54/D56/D63.)
- A cancellation accepted before the guarded dispatch transaction commits yields zero PSP sends. The execution CAS and collection permit commit together. For an unadmitted step or one that execution proves unsent, collection records a terminal closure and ledger releases the settlement association through its append-only no-effect receipt. A committed permit keeps in-flight and unknown outcomes recoverable. (Execution, collection and ledger under REQ-CON-07, REQ-FAIL-19 and D63/F04.)
- A late capture remains an actual fact. Compensation requires a separate refund request tied to the original operation, route, merchant and remaining capacity. Execution uses the original binding for admission, dispatch and recovery of the refund. The collection permit stays with the original charge. (Host, execution and ledger under REQ-FAIL-11, REQ-OWN-19 and D57/D63.)
- Annual funding and the host's twelve monthly grant occurrences share coverage correlation. Host grant retries reuse the grant occurrence and receipt. Billium keys monetary occurrences by component, purpose and frozen interval, so funding replays and schedule revisions retain one obligation. (Host, schedule, rating and ledger under REQ-ACC-08, REQ-ACC-20, REQ-NBR-06, INV-11 and D56.)
- The bundle proration selection in `fixtures/vectors/collection/bundle-proration.fixture` uses `RatingCorrection` with the original component price, October coverage and Europe/Berlin local-day policy. The fixture selects the original tax rate, a 15/31 retained fraction and a 1,920 minor-unit credit and refund on component A. The host supplies the amendment from the frozen agreement. Rating issues the linked correction through ledger and execution processes the separately authorized refund. The sibling's allocation and issued invoice lines are preserved. The host retains consumed rights and provisioning receipts. (Host, rating, ledger and execution under COV-AC-373, COV-AC-511, COV-AC-546, REQ-OWN-19, INV-12 and D56/D57.)
